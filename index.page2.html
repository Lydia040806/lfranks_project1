The Odyssey

The Odyssey is an ancient Greek epic traditionally attributed to the poet Homer. It follows Odysseus, king of Ithaca, as he struggles to return home after the Trojan War. His journey lasts many years and takes him across dangerous seas, where he and his crew encounter powerful forces and unfamiliar lands. The sea god Poseidon becomes an obstacle, while the goddess Athena offers help. Odysseus must rely on courage, patience, and clever thinking to survive.

While Odysseus is away, his wife Penelope and son Telemachus face pressure from suitors who want to take over his household. Penelope remains loyal and uses her own cleverness to delay them, while Telemachus grows into his role as a leader. When Odysseus finally reaches Ithaca, he returns in disguise and carefully reveals himself to those he trusts. The poem explores homecoming, loyalty, hospitality, and perseverance. Its many adventures have influenced storytelling for centuries.
